📚 About Vedic Mathematics

Vedic Mathematics is a system of mathematical techniques that focuses on making calculations faster, simpler, and more intuitive. It uses a collection of methods known as Sutras, which can be applied to arithmetic, multiplication, division, squares, percentages, algebra, and many other calculations. Vedic Mathematics encourages students to understand patterns and relationships between numbers instead of depending only on lengthy traditional calculation methods. With regular practice, students can improve their calculation speed, accuracy, concentration, and confidence.
